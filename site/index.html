How it works

Three things a normal gym does not do

Every gym in Colombo has weights. These are the parts that decide whether you are still coming in three months.

It names the weight

Open the app and the first thing you see is a number. It came from your own last session, not a chart on a wall. If you finished every set in range, it goes up. If you did not, it holds you there until you do.

You can see every day you did

Tap any date and the whole session comes back. Every set, every weight, every rep. Your best lift on each exercise sits at the top so you always know what you are chasing.

The desk notices before you drift

If you stop turning up, somebody here knows within days and gets in touch. Not a mass message. The person at the desk can see it is you and how long it has been.

Before you ask

The questions everybody asks at the door

Do I need to know how to train already?

No. The app gives you the session and the weight for every set. If you have never lifted before you start at the bar and it moves you up from there, one small step at a time, based on what you actually managed.

What happens if I miss a week?

Nothing is lost. Your plan waits. When you come back the app puts you on the weight you last completed, not the one you were building towards, so the first session back is one you can finish.

Can I train without a trainer?

Yes, and most people here do. The app covers the part a trainer does most often, which is telling you what to load and when to move up. A trainer is worth adding when you want somebody watching how you move.

What does it cost?

The plans above come straight out of the gym's own system, so what you are reading is what is being charged today. If the desk changes a price this morning, this page says the new one this afternoon.

Is my training data private?

Your sessions are yours. The desk can see whether you have been coming, which is how they know to check on you, and your trainer sees your training if you are on a plan with one. Progress photos are private to you unless you share one.
